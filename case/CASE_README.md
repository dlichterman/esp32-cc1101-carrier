# 3D-printed case for the ESP32 + CC1101 carrier

Parts: `case_base.stl` (tray with 4 standoffs) and `case_lid.stl` (lid with 4 screw pillars). STEP files and `case_assembly.step` (base + lid + PCB) are included.
Outer size 101.8 x 61.8 x 29.6 mm. Everything is generated by `make_case.py` (CadQuery); edit the parameters at the top and re-run to change any dimension.

## Hardware
- 4 x M3 heat-set inserts, about 4.6 mm OD x 5.7 mm long (pressed into the standoffs; hole is 4.2 mm, 6 mm deep).
- 4 x M3 x 25 mm pan-head screws. They go down through the lid, the lid pillars, the board's corner holes and into the inserts.

## Assembly
1. Melt the inserts into the four standoff holes of the base.
2. Plug both modules into their sockets on the board, then lower the whole board into the tray. The USB connector faces the front wall; the SMA connector drops into the open slot in the right wall.
3. Put the lid on (its rim sits just inside the walls) and tighten the four screws.
4. Screw the antenna onto the SMA connector through the slot.

## Print settings
- Base: print floor-down, no supports. Lid: print upside down (flat top face on the bed, pillars pointing up), no supports.
- 0.2 mm layers, 3-4 walls, about 25% infill. PETG or ASA is better than PLA near the ESP32 regulator; PLA is fine for a bench case.

If any of these are different, change the matching parameter in `make_case.py` and re-run.

## C3
Rev B moves C3 2.5 mm toward the top of the board, so its 5 mm body (y 12.5 to 17.5 mm) is about 0.8 mm clear of the CC1101 module's edge (about y 18.3 mm, measured from photos). It can stand upright (11.5 mm, under the 18 mm lid). On a rev A board, C3 sits partly under the module: lay it on its side with the body pointing toward the top of the board.
